Thank you for using Pulse.

This version replaces the robot in the rings with Agent Robot Avatar, an original character by CX ArtLab released under the MIT License. My thanks to them.

The robot is off by default. To use it, turn on **Robot in the ring** for an account in Settings, where you can also choose its colour, personality and shape.

If you have any questions or suggestions, please let me know on [GitHub](https://github.com/qunqin24/Pulse/issues).
