# Decisions

Why something is the way it is, what failed, and measurements taken at the time. **Historical, not newly verified.** Current behaviour lives in the topic docs; link here instead of retelling the whole story.

A decision records a reason, not proof the measurement still holds — a local probe logged here once disagreed with real input, so do not promote one to a verified claim. Evidence levels and what a probe cannot show: [../../CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md) (Evidence levels), [../testing.md](../testing.md).
